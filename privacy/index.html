Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

sudoku42 stores an anonymous account ID and the game data needed to run the app: game IDs, moves, notes, puzzle difficulty, and timelines. This lets you play, sync, and view your games. This data is stored with Google Firebase.

If you join a multiplayer game, the app can send notifications about activity in that game using Firebase Cloud Messaging.

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner and rewarded ads. Google may collect information about your device and ad interactions to show and measure ads. You can read more in the Google Privacy Policy and Google’s AdMob information for app users.

iOS waitlist

Before the iPhone and iPad launch, you could join a waitlist with your email address. We stored your email address, signup time, consent version, campaign source, and a coarse device category (such as iPhone, iPad, Android, or desktop) with Google Firebase, only to email you about the launch. We did not use the list for a newsletter or sell it.

Shared links included a campaign tag; they did not include your email address or a personal ID. To request deletion of your waitlist entry, email manuel@sudoku42.com. We delete the waitlist after the launch notification.
